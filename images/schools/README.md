# University emblems

These marks identify the universities in the owner's education history. The downloaded artwork is unmodified; CSS preserves its aspect ratio and displays it on white in both themes.

- `pku-emblem.png`: the red PNG from Peking University's official [circular emblem download](https://vim.pku.edu.cn/docs/20240527144201516364.zip), linked by its [Visual Identity Office](https://vim.pku.edu.cn/xzzq/).
- `hkust-emblem.svg`: HKUST's blue-and-gold university emblem, obtained from the [Wikipedia file page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology_symbol.svg) ([original SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/8/85/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology_symbol.svg)). The university describes the emblem on its [Our Story page](https://hkust.edu.hk/our-story). The official logo download service was unavailable during retrieval.

University marks remain the property of their respective institutions; no separate license for the marks is granted by this repository.
